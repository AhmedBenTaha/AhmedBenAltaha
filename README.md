## About Me

I'm an **AI Engineer & Data Scientist** focused on turning LLM capabilities into systems that actually hold up in production — not just demos. My work centers on **RAG, AI agents, LLM engineering, and machine learning**, with a growing focus on the parts of AI that get skipped when people just call an API: evaluation, guardrails, and observability.

I care about the engineering *around* the model as much as the model itself — retrieval quality, context design, failure modes, and making sure a system stays reliable once real users touch it. Alongside AI engineering, I also work in **data science and analytics** — using data to inform decisions, not just power models.
