I am an **AI Engineer & Data Scientist** focused on building practical, reliable, and production-ready AI systems. My work centers on the engineering side of artificial intelligence—designing robust architectures around Large Language Models rather than simply making API calls. 

I specialize in **LLM Engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI Agents, and Production AI Architecture**. I enjoy bridging the gap between cutting-edge research and real-world utility, ensuring that generative AI capabilities are grounded, evaluated, and scalable.
